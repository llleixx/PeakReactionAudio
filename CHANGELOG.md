# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- Added reactions: before passing out, helping, being helped, falling, hard landing, waking up, and revival after death.
- Mixed reaction sounds into normal game voice chat, with local playback and push-to-talk microphone gating.
- Added per-event sound folders, random selection, volume controls, and independent 3-second default cooldowns.
- Included Orga's pass-out warning and Tom's falling scream; other event folders are empty for custom sounds.
- Added WAV, Ogg Vorbis, and MP3 loading.
